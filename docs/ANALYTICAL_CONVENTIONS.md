# Analytical conventions

| Area | Bound or convention |
| --- | --- |
| Import | One flat table; 25 MiB file; 50,000 rows; 80 fields; 80 MiB decoded table; chunked CSV guard |
| Missing values | Empty cells by default; literal `NA` preserved; optional explicit tokens; failed numeric parsing is counted |
| Numeric inference | Recipe 2: at least 95% parseable, with identifier-name strings, leading-zero codes and integer strings beyond 2^53 protected before numeric coercion. Literal marker/placeholder/sentinel candidates are counted before declared missing-token replacement. Recipe 1 remains available for exact historical replay |
| Statistics | Linear quantiles, sample standard deviation (`ddof=1`), pairwise finite correlations, explicit support |
| Semantic profiling | At most eight automatic ID entities, ten dimensions, eighty retained relationship proposals; exact checks over the bounded family; no exhaustive dependency claim |
| Semantic metrics | Typed metric algebra; at most two group fields and twenty displayed groups; explicit full-family recipes allow up to 200 original parts; Other recomputes source components; source definitions and scope are recorded |
| Observation process | Preserve by default; compatible confirmed fill policy; at most 200 source series, 20,000 expanded cells and 1,200 output periods; explicit NULL is not an absent observation |
| Discovery | Up to 300 numeric pairs; 24 conditional-shape screens; 12 automatic findings; at most 18 interactive working views, with pinned snapshots preserved; seeded 20,000-row association sample; up to 5,000 visible scatter points |
| Groups | At least 20 paired rows/group for distribution comparisons; 30 for subgroup correlations; at most eight displayed groups, with omissions recorded |
| Views | Thirteen constrained templates; at most 1,200 combined temporal marks or ECDF vertices including endpoint extensions. ECDF observed vertices retain exact cumulative values; compression has a certified maximum vertical error |
| Chart constraints | Square inner scatter/density areas with independent data units; magnitude bars include zero; equal-width histograms preserve exact numeric intervals; signed residual colours centre on zero. Native and review renderers share quantities, domains, orders and grouped colours |
| Distribution contrasts | Two-group frequency polygons share 5–50 pooled full-source bins and use within-group proportions with separate sample sizes. Quantile-deviation markers use the five saved full-source estimates, with horizontal zero and no invented interpolation or paired-row membership |
| Contribution display | Treemaps require a complete, nonnegative additive partition and positive recorded whole; no pooled Other or overlapping full-credit attribution. The editable initial choice uses a panel row budget, not a universal item-count threshold. Zero-area parts remain in exact values |
| Comparisons | Two named child contexts; up to three saved boards plus an active board; companions count toward the 18 working-view allowance. Row/entity membership, reference overlap, denominators and anchored allocation remain explicit |
| Facets and density | At most four facets, eight items and 32 intersections; shared metric scale. Numeric density uses a complete 4–24 by 4–24 equal-width grid with exact counts and edges |
| Temporal companions | Full-window trailing means of eligible period amounts/counts; at least two complete calendar cycles; common-baseline indexing requires compatible units/basis and positive complete values at one reference date |
| Challenges | Up to three automatic checks on three leading findings plus up to two additional metric views; Terra may add at most two eligible checks |
| Models | At most 10,000 labeled rows; 40 predictors; bounded encoding and model iterations; ordinary 70/30 split; two experiments/context. One preparation path for preview and execution; target-dependent screens and preprocessing use training rows. Mean/median regression baselines, per-model held-out predictions and explicit comparison verdicts |
| Task and availability | Dataset-scoped descriptive/diagnostic/prospective task, explicit target, unit, population description, decision time and optional horizon. Population prose is not a filter. Prospective predictors require reviewed availability; known-at-anchor declarations bind to the decision-time basis. Late or unknown timestamp-based values exclude the whole predictor; undocumented mutable snapshots remain diagnostic assumptions or prospective exclusions |
| Temporal evaluation | Forward-window targets require an anchor, declared complete-through timestamp and chronological holdout. Incomplete outcome windows are omitted; training outcomes not known by the held-out boundary are purged. UTC arithmetic; naive timestamps are treated as a common UTC clock and require source-basis review. These checks do not reconstruct historical values |
| Recording coverage | Full active-context denominators for up to twelve fields; day/month/year/decade bins chosen to bound the period family, up to eight recorded group levels and two categorical fields with at most twelve levels each. Missing dates are reported separately. A ≥0.25 range in missing shares across observed periods is an engineering review trigger, not proof of leakage or a collection change |
| Event lab | One event table; recurrence or event-count recipes; 3–6 chronological, nonoverlapping anchors; 1–365 day horizon/lookback, with lookback at least horizon; ≤10,000 derived entity-anchor rows; two preparations and two model evaluations/context, one evaluation/frozen task. Past-only population and available-event features; forward labels require explicit complete-coverage attestation. Last complete anchor held out; training windows plus an explicit 0–365-day outcome reporting-delay bound must finish before it. Observed delays exceeding the bound reject preparation; previous-window comparator retained. Exact source membership and a distinct derived schema; no generated SQL, joins or arbitrary recipes |
| Providers | Standard Jev provider: eight requests, 256 questions and 384,000 conservative serialized-input budget bytes; each typed request: 40 questions and 48,000 bytes. Terra: three requests per provider instance; automatic loop normally uses two, plus an optional separate semantic review and one bounded comparison review per investigation. One TabPFN fit/predict per experiment |
| Judgment policy | Noul yes ≥0.7 / no ≤0.3; otherwise unresolved. Choice requires top probability ≥0.7 and margin ≥0.15; companion applicability must also resolve yes. Relevance Score uses three anchored levels, maximum level probability ≥0.6 and a two-endpoint ambiguity guard. These are uncalibrated application defaults, not measured correctness guarantees |
| Recommendations | Up to sixteen unique eligible candidates; Python combines 0.55 relevance + 0.25 family complementarity + 0.15 support − 0.05 cost. Unresolved model relevance retains deterministic order. Four explicit relevance reviews/context; no model output grants eligibility |
| Clarification and claims | Metric dependency/eligibility counts are computed, not information-gain estimates. Note reviews: two/context, 4,000 characters, twelve supplied lines. Hypothetical quantities use isolated copies. Terra claim review: up to six atomic claims with cited evidence, independent support/scope Nouls; literal numeric/causal guards and incomplete-check flags remain in Python |
| Text coding | Six frozen binary features, 240 selected source rows, 2,000 characters/text; four distinct texts/batch; duplicate text reuses answers but preserves row membership. Two coding runs/context. Per-run provider budget: 64 requests, 1,440 questions, 2,000,000 conservative input-budget bytes; whole-plan preflight before any coding request |
| Text proposals and models | Two Terra proposals/context from up to eight text-only samples without targets; two matched predictive experiments/context. Original permitted predictors versus original-plus-coded features share exact train/test memberships; questions are frozen beforehand. Same-estimator metric differences are explicit. Unknown/missing/overlong are distinct predictor states; source availability, decision-time basis and entity holdout declarations carry over |
| Visual review | Three PNGs; 1.5 MiB/image; at most 600 plotted records/image; 48,000 text characters/request; reduced views have matching tables and scope. Aggregate cells/time intervals are never randomly omitted; further CDF compression records its additional error; virtual endpoint extensions carry no source membership |
| Execution | Two admitted workers globally; one task/session; 360-second application deadline; finite provider transport/poll timeouts; five follow-up questions/context |
| Reproducibility | Seed 20261120 for analysis; examples declare their own seed; exact source bytes, parser, semantics and row membership recorded |

Cancellation detaches from remote results and stops later stages; an already accepted remote operation can remain billable. No provider retry starts a fresh remote fit automatically. A chronological split is an exploration protocol, not a guarantee of future performance; a row holdout does not establish independence. The automatic shortlist does not attach selected-winner p-values or causal conclusions. Ranking weights, thresholds and budgets are visible engineering defaults.

The [chart design audit](research/chart-design-audit.md) separates deterministic Python constraints from task-dependent Jev/Terra guidance. A versioned policy accompanies provider requests and recorded decisions; model preferences cannot grant an operation permission or change a calculation.

Semantic declarations belong to the exact uploaded table. The observed interval is not an expected schedule. Business meaning, weight/exposure and recording completeness require human knowledge. A selected-row context does not inherit the whole table’s gap-fill coverage. Ratios compose untransformed, non-temporal component metrics; temporal snapshot/counter nesting is not supported. State snapshots aggregate latest entity observations, not sums of daily stock levels. Currency bases are recorded without automatic conversion. Circular means require degrees and a defined resultant. No join, external entity resolution, holiday calendar, joint group/time split, causal model or lineage system is implied.
