## Operational lessons retained with qualification

- **Literal values precede interpretation.** Exact source markers and identifier spelling are evidence. Numeric/string sentinel lists suggest review; domain knowledge establishes whether they represent missingness. A string beyond floating-point precision stays text rather than silently changing identity.
- **Event time and information availability differ.** A row's timestamp alone does not establish all exported attributes were known then. Undocumented latest snapshots cannot be made historical through a model prompt.
- **A complete outcome window defines an eligible label.** A future zero requires complete recording for that window and population. Recent incomplete windows are omitted, not negative examples. Coverage dates are attestations, not inferred maxima.
- **Population selection uses the past.** Eligibility must not depend on the future event that defines the label. Finite recipes test strict/inclusive boundaries and future-perturbation invariance of features.
- **Diagnostics motivate questions.** Large missingness contrasts and near-deterministic associations can arise from legitimate mechanisms. They do not prove leakage, causal structure or recording error. Zero values, duplicate rows and last-edit fields are not universally discarded.
- **Compare representation within an estimator.** If both data access and estimator change, their performance difference cannot isolate the added information's contribution. First Look reports same-estimator text ablations separately.
- **Preserve negative comparisons.** A weak score establishes only what was observed under the recorded protocol. It does not establish that no useful signal exists or justify repeatedly tuning questions against the final holdout.

Python enforces these bounded patterns; Terra can propose supported tasks/explanations/checks; Jev judges narrow supplied evidence and relevance; tabular models evaluate permitted predictive inputs; humans supply missing operational facts. No model supplies authenticated history, completeness or business semantics.
